00_project_setup_and_data_inventory.ipynb

Load the files from the provided data\student_data folder.
Check shapes, column types, date ranges, missing values, duplicates, and key uniqueness.
Record assumptions and produce a file/row inventory.
01_data_cleaning_and_quality_report.ipynb

Trust Date over Year and Month; standardize labels and dates.
Deduplicate Record_ID, keeping the latest Load_Timestamp.
Check quantities, prices, revenue, unit/currency inconsistencies, and master-data conflicts.
Save cleaned datasets and a machine-readable report of issues, affected rows, and corrections.
02_stockouts_and_demand_reconstruction.ipynb

Join inventory_stockouts.csv to the sales history.
Estimate unconstrained demand where shipment totals were limited; keep observed sales separate from adjusted demand.
Document uncertainty and avoid treating missing sales rows as zero demand.
03_demand_exploration_and_anomalies.ipynb

Explore trends, seasonality, zero-sales frequency, order cycles, customer changes, and SKU lifecycle.
Flag unusual observations and classify them as likely errors or real events.
Produce an anomaly register with reason and action.
04_features_and_external_drivers.ipynb

Create calendar, lag, rolling, intermittency, lifecycle, event, and stockout-related features.
Explore macro-indicator relationships and plausible lead/lag effects by product family.
Ensure every forecast feature would actually be available for the forecast period.
05_baselines_and_backtesting.ipynb

Build at least two simple baselines, such as seasonal naïve and recent-history average.
Use time-based rolling-origin validation, including a 19-month forecast horizon.
Report WAPE and bias by family, region, and horizon—not just one overall score.
06_advanced_forecast_model.ipynb

Compare a global machine-learning model with the baselines.
Test an intermittent-demand approach for sparse SKUs.
Select models based on backtest performance, not training fit; explain where each model works best.
07_final_forecast_and_submission.ipynb

Refit the selected approach using all appropriate history.
Forecast every row in test.csv.
Check row coverage, duplicate keys, blanks, non-negative integer values, and the required submission columns.
Export the final submission file.
08_uncertainty_and_business_checks.ipynb
Estimate empirical P10/P50/P90 forecast ranges from rolling-origin residuals.
Check hierarchy rollups, product lifecycle, known events, and forecast coverage.
Document procurement inputs that are unavailable; do not invent reorder quantities.

09_local_dashboard.ipynb
Generate a standalone local dashboard with shared family, SKU, region, channel, and customer filters.
Include executive overview, data health, demand explorer, anomaly monitor, and forecast/accuracy views.
Show continuous historical-to-forecast units and estimated revenue, lifecycle, uncertainty, and WAPE/bias.
